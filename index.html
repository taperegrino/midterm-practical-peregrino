About Us

We are a fashion brand that focuses on trendy, high-quality streetwear. Our main products include casual apparel and accessories designed forself-expression and individuality for our users.

"Stunna" is derived from a hip-hop slang, short for "Stunner," meaning this is someone who expressed themselves with style, confidence, money, or success. (Example: Young Stunna). The slang is most commonly used amongst Gen Z and young millennials. The 'PH' stands for expressing its Filipino-based authentic and inclusive brand
